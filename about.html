Our Story & Legacy

25+ Years of Clinical Integrity & Healing

Discover the founding philosophy, clinical leadership, and international quality standards that have made Dhawan Hospital a beacon of trust for over two decades.

Founding Philosophy

A Vision to Transform Healthcare Delivery

Dhawan Hospital was founded with an unyielding conviction: that world-class, ethical, and technologically advanced medical care should be delivered with deep empathy and transparent affordability.

Under the visionary guidance of veteran surgeons Dr. R. K. Dhawan and Dr. Sunita Dhawan, the institution pioneered advanced joint replacements and high-risk maternal healthcare in the region, earning the trust of over 100,000 families across North India.

Our Mission Deliver patient-first clinical precision through advanced surgical technology and relentless hygiene.
Our Vision To remain the foremost centre of excellence for Joint Care, Minimally Invasive Surgery & Critical Care.
Evolution of Excellence

Milestones in Our 25-Year Journey

A continuous pursuit of clinical advancement, cutting-edge technology, and patient satisfaction.

1998

Foundation of Dhawan Hospital

Inaugurated as a dedicated surgical centre focusing on Orthopaedics and Women's Health with 25 beds and state-of-the-art OT facilities.

2008

Modular Laminar Flow OT & Critical Care Wing

Expanded to a 100-bed multi-speciality tertiary facility with Class-100 laminar airflow surgical theatres and 24/7 Level-III ICU.

2016

NABH & ISO Accreditation Conferred

Awarded National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers (NABH) certification for exceptional patient safety and clinical protocols.

2024

Robotic Joint Replacement & 128-Slice CT Wing

Integrated robotic-assisted knee and hip arthroplasty, 128-Slice Multi-Detector CT scanner, and digital catheterization lab support.
