Medical & Surgical Specialities

Centres of Clinical Excellence

Explore our multi-disciplinary medical departments, advanced surgical protocols, specialized OPD clinics, and leading consultant specialists.

Orthopaedics & Joint Replacement

Internationally recognized surgical centre for Robotic Total Knee & Hip Replacement, arthroscopic ligament reconstruction, and complex poly-trauma.

• Robotic & Navigated Total Knee Arthroplasty (TKR) • Primary & Revision Total Hip Arthroplasty (THR) • Arthroscopic Keyhole ACL/PCL/Meniscus Repairs • In-house Post-Surgical Physiotherapy & Rehab
Chief: Dr. R. K. Dhawan Book OPD

Cardiology & Heart Institute

Comprehensive cardiovascular care including 2D Echocardiography, Stress Thallium, TMT, Holter monitoring, acute coronary triage, and heart failure clinics.

• High-Resolution 2D/3D Color Doppler Echo • Treadmill Stress Testing (TMT) & 24-hr Holter • Preventive Hypertension & Lipidology Clinic • Post-Angioplasty & Bypass Rehabilitation
Chief: Dr. Arvind Mehta Book OPD

Laparoscopic & GI Surgery

Pioneering minimally invasive keyhole procedures for gallbladder stones, complex abdominal hernias, appendicitis, and gastrointestinal tumors.

• Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder Removal) • Laparoscopic TEP/TAPP Inguinal & Ventral Hernia • Laser Proctology for Piles, Fissure & Fistula • Gastrointestinal & Colorectal Resections
Senior: Dr. Priya Sharma Book OPD

Obstetrics, Gynaecology & IVF

Holistic women's wellness covering high-risk maternal delivery, painless epidural labor, total laparoscopic hysterectomy (TLH), and infertility evaluation.

• High-Risk Pregnancy & Painless Delivery Suites • Total Laparoscopic Hysterectomy (TLH) & Myomectomy • Infertility Evaluation, Follicular Tracking & IUI • Adolescent & Menopausal Health Management
Senior: Dr. Sunita Dhawan Book OPD

Neurology & Micro-Spine Care

Advanced neurological diagnostic wing and surgical management for acute ischemic stroke, epilepsy, sciatica, disc herniations, and spinal stenosis.

• 24/7 Acute Ischemic Stroke Thrombolysis Unit • Microscopic Lumbar & Cervical Discectomy • Digital Video EEG, EMG & Nerve Conduction Studies • Parkinson's, Tremor & Movement Disorder Clinic
Consultant: Dr. Vikram Sen Book OPD

Level-III Intensive Care (ICU/CCU)

Round-the-clock intensivist coverage for severe sepsis, ARDS, polytrauma resuscitation, cardiac arrest recovery, and complex post-operative monitoring.

• Dedicated 1:1 Specialist Critical Care Nursing • Invasive Arterial & Central Venous Pressure Monitoring • Bedside Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy (CRRT) • Ultra-Modern HEPA-14 Clean Air Environments
Chief: Dr. Ananya Verma Book OPD
